PLAINTIFFS’ RENEWED MOTION FOR PARTIAL SUMMARY JUDGMENT 
ON DIRECT COPYRIGHT INFRINGEMENT AND RELATED DEFENSES 
 
Pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 56, Plaintiffs Thomson Reuters Enterprise 
Centre GmbH and West Publishing Corporation (collectively, “Plaintiffs”) respectfully move for 
entry of partial summary judgment in favor of Plaintiffs on their direct copyright infringement 
claim against Defendant ROSS Intelligence Inc. (“ROSS”), as well as ROSS’s defenses of merger, 
scènes à faire, innocent infringement, and copyright misuse. 
The grounds for this motion are set forth in Plaintiffs’ opening brief and supporting exhibits 
and declarations, filed concurrently.  A proposed order is attached.
