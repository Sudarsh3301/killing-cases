Plaintiff, Christopher Matthew Batten, by and through undersigned counsel, 
and pursuant to Rule 56 of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and Local Rule 
56.1B, submits this Statement of Additional Disputed Material Facts.  
1. Matthew Batten was involved in a horrific motorcycle crash in 2014. (Batten 
Affidavit1 ¶ 2). 
2. The crash caused Mr. Batten to need dozens of surgeries to install hardware 
and screws throughout the right side of his body. (Batten Affidavit ¶ 3).

4. Mr. Batten has a neurostimulator implant for pain. (Batten Dep2. 59:12-60:6). 
5. Matthew Batten needs a job with a chair and the ability to sit down because 
of his condition. (Batten Dep. 20:24-21:19).
