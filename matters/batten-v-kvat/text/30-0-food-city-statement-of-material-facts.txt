Pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 56, the Defendant herein,   
K-VA-T Food Stores, Inc, d/b/a Food City (“Food City”) has MOVED this 
Honorable Court for summary judgment as to all of the allegations in the Complaint.

undisputed and support Food City’s Motion for Summary Judgment. All references 
are to Depositions filed as Exhibits with Defendant Food City’s Motion:

Plaintiff, Christopher Matthew Batten (“Plaintiff”) began his

Store in Ooltewah, Tennessee. (Ex. 1, Plaintiff’s depo., p. 22, lines 21-23; p. 37, 
lines 13-18).
